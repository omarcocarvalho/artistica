# M3 — Composition lines: Overview & Shared Contracts

> **Approved by the owner on 2026-10-07** (PR #102), with every recommended default in "Questions for the owner" accepted.

> **Status:** draft for owner review. Nothing in M3 starts until the owner approves this plan and answers (or accepts the defaults of) the **Questions for the owner** at the end.

> **For agentic workers:** this is the umbrella document for M3. It is **not** executed on its own. M3 is split into four sub-plans (A–D below), each executed with superpowers:subagent-driven-development, exactly like M1 and M2. Every sub-plan treats the **Shared contracts** section of this file as binding. A sub-plan that needs a contract change lists it under "Contract change requests" instead of silently diverging.

**Goal:** Ship `v0.3.0`. Each image can carry composition lines — grid (N × M), rule of thirds, diagonals and armature, golden ratio lines, golden spiral (start corner chosen), centre lines — each off by default, with a per-image colour, thickness and opacity, and an "Apply to all" of its own. Lines are drawn on top of the image, on every printed version of it, as **vector paths** in the PDF. The preview and the PDF draw the same line geometry from the same page model, so the lines in the PDF match the preview exactly and stay sharp at any zoom.

**Spec:** `docs/spec.md` §2.7 (Composition lines), §2.5 ("lines and marks drawn as vectors"), §2.8 (AI lines, M4: the boundary), §2.9 ("default study and line settings" are remembered), §2.12 (phone flow), §3 (non-functional), §4 ("`PageModel` describing image placements, bleed, crop marks and line segments"; "`lines/` composition-line geometry (pure TS)"), §7 ("Lines: geometry tests (thirds at 1/3 and 2/3, spiral within bounds, etc.)"; "PDF tests … check … that the vector lines are present"), §8 "M3 — Composition lines", Q&A R8, Q29, Q31, design answers D1 (Lines inside the phone Studies step) and D7 (Studies and Lines each have their own "apply to all"). **Design:** `design/lines.html` (desktop Lines tab and line overlays), `design/mobile-flow.html` (collapsible Lines section in the Studies step), `design/studies.html` / `design/workspace.html` (the same tab markup), `design/mockup.css` (`.tile__lines`: stroke in mm, opacity), `design/README.md`. **Conventions:** `CLAUDE.md`. **Constraints carried over:** the M1 and M2 overviews' contracts and rulings, the ledgers (`docs/superpowers/ledgers/m1-*.md`, `m2.md`) and `HANDOVER.md`.

## M4 boundary (spec §2.8)

M3 does **not** build edge/contour outlines, face construction lines, pose lines, model downloads, the "Guides from the photo" section of `design/lines.html`, or any per-pixel line detection. It leaves these seams, and nothing more:

- `LineSettings` is a plain object copied whole by the store, "Apply to all", the settings schema and `linesKey`. M4 adds its fields (e.g. `edge`, `face`, `pose`) with a settings migration (v3 → v4), without changing M3's fields.
- Lines live in the **picture frame** (the image as edited: after crop, rotation and flips; M3-R2), and `frameToPage` maps that frame onto a tile. M4 adds a `sourceToFrame` mapping for landmarks found in source pixels and reuses `frameToPage` and the `TileLines` page-model block, so its lines inherit the clip, the turn, the "every version" rule and both renderers.
- `TileLines.strokes` is a list of stroke batches; M4 adds batches (its own style rules are M4's decision).
- `LinesPanel` is a stack of sections; M4 appends its section after "Composition" and before "Line style". M3 renders no placeholder for it.

## Rulings made in this plan (technical, not product)

Each ruling is binding for the sub-plans. They are technical consequences of the spec and of the owner questions' recommended defaults (marked). A reviewer who disagrees raises a contract change request.

- **M3-R1 Line settings live on the image, not in `ImageEdits` or `StudySettings`.** `ImageDescriptor` gains `lines: LineSettings`. Crop/rotate/copies stay in `edits`, studies in `study`; "Apply to all" for lines copies `lines` only (D7).
- **M3-R2 Line geometry is defined in the picture frame.** The frame is the printed picture as the user sees it after crop, rotation and flips: width × height = the tile's trim in mm, swapped when the engine turned the item. Edits never move lines relative to the picture's edges ("top left" is the top left of the picture as edited); the engine's 90° turn rotates the lines **with** the picture onto the page (owner Q4, default).
- **M3-R3 Frame → page.** Not turned: `(u, v) → (trim.x + u, trim.y + v)`. Turned (90° clockwise, as `combineRotation`): `(u, v) → (trim.x + trim.w − v, trim.y + u)`, with frame `w = trim.h`, `h = trim.w`. Bézier control points map with the same affine map, so curves stay exact.
- **M3-R4 Lines are clipped to the trim.** They never draw into the bleed, the gutter or across crop marks (owner Q5, default). The clip is the tile's trim rect in both renderers.
- **M3-R5 Every version tile of an image gets the same lines** (spec §2.7), each in its own tile's frame. Lines are not part of `tileRenderKey`: switching lines never re-renders, re-encodes or re-embeds tile pixels, and never re-runs study jobs.
- **M3-R6 One page model, two renderers, one geometry.** `buildPageModels` emits `PageModel.lines: TileLines[]` with every path already in page millimetres. The canvas preview and the PDF composer replay those paths 1:1 (moves, lines, cubic Béziers); neither computes geometry. Curves are cubic Béziers in the model (never flattened), so the PDF stays exact at any zoom.
- **M3-R7 One stroke per batch.** Per tile, all solid paths are one stroke and all dashed paths a second stroke, in that order. A path stroked once never darkens where its own subpaths cross or coincide (e.g. a 3 × 3 grid over the rule of thirds) at opacity < 100%, in canvas and PDF alike. Where a dashed centre line crosses a solid line, both renderers blend the two strokes the same way.
- **M3-R8 Stroke state is explicit and identical.** Colour `#rrggbb` (PDF `DeviceRGB`, `RG`), opacity as `globalAlpha` / an ExtGState `/CA` (omitted at 100%), width in mm, butt caps, miter joins, miter limit 10, dash pattern in mm with phase 0. Lines are drawn after every tile and before the crop marks (both renderers), so marks stay on top and lines sit on the pictures.
- **M3-R9 Preview line width floor.** The preview draws a line at `max(1 device px, widthMm × pxPerMm)`, exactly as M1 draws crop marks; geometry and dash lengths are never floored. On paper and in the PDF the width is exact. ("Match the preview exactly" is about geometry, position and colour; a 0.1 mm line cannot be thinner than one screen pixel.)
- **M3-R10 Golden spiral construction.** The spiral is the classic golden spiral of quarter arcs in a golden rectangle, scaled to the tile (owner Q2, default: it fills the tile, so it lines up with the golden-ratio lines; quarter circles become quarter ellipses on a non-golden tile). Each quarter arc is one cubic Bézier with κ = 4/3·(√2 − 1) (radial error < 0.03% of the radius). `SPIRAL_ARCS = 12` arcs (the last arc's radius is φ⁻¹¹ ≈ 0.5% of the short side). The outer arc starts **at the chosen corner and leaves it along the short edge**, as in `design/lines.html` (owner Q3, default); for a 300 × 400 frame starting top right, the arc end points are (300, 0) → (0, 247.2) → (185.4, 400) → (300, 305.6) → (229.2, 247.2) → (185.4, 283.3) …, exactly the mockup's polyline. A square tile is treated as landscape.
- **M3-R11 Golden-ratio lines** are at `1/φ²` and `1/φ` (≈ 0.382 and 0.618) of the width and of the height (the mockup's 114.6 / 185.4 of 300 and 152.8 / 247.2 of 400).
- **M3-R12 Armature** (owner Q1, default): the two diagonals, the eight lines from each corner to the midpoints of the two sides that do not touch it, and the rhombus through the four side midpoints — the symmetric form of the mockup's drawing (which shows six of the eight corner lines). The centre cross belongs to "Centre lines".
- **M3-R13 Grid** `cols × rows` divides the frame into equal cells: `cols − 1` vertical and `rows − 1` horizontal interior lines (never on the frame edge); 1 × 1 draws nothing. Columns run along the picture's width as edited.
- **M3-R14 Centre lines are dashed** (owner Q10, default, as mocked); every other type is solid. Dash = `[max(1.5, 6w), max(1, 4w)]` mm for a width `w` mm (the mockup's 6 : 4 ratio, floored so thin lines still read as dashed).
- **M3-R15 Canonical order.** Types are generated in `COMPOSITION_LINE_TYPES` order (`grid, thirds, armature, golden, spiral, centre`), each type's subpaths in a fixed order; the same settings give the same page model and the same PDF bytes.
- **M3-R16 Line settings are sanitised in one place.** `sanitizeLines` owns every range: grid 1–20 (integer), width 0.10–2.00 mm rounded to 0.05, opacity 10–100 % (integer), colour `#rrggbb` lowercased (anything else → the default colour), corner one of four (else the default). The settings Zod schema checks **types only** (the M2 B2 ruling), so a stored value is normalised exactly as the store would.
- **M3-R17 Duplicates are ranked by what they print, lines included.** `build-items` ranks identical photos by their studies, **then** by their `linesKey`, so two copies of one photo with different lines give the same layout in any input order. With no lines anywhere the ranking (and the golden snapshot) is unchanged.
- **M3-R18 No new runtime dependencies.** Line geometry is written here (small, pure, tested). pdf-lib's low-level operators already ship. No `package.json` change in any M3 task.
- **M3-R19 Lines are print content, not guides.** They show whether "Guides" is on or off.
- **M3-R20 The page description names the lines.** `describePage` adds each tile's active line types, so a screen-reader user hears what prints ("pears.jpg, 120 × 90 mm, lines: Rule of thirds, Centre lines").

## Shared contracts (binding)

### `src/shared/model/lines.ts` (A1)

```ts
export const COMPOSITION_LINE_TYPES = ['grid', 'thirds', 'armature', 'golden', 'spiral', 'centre'] as const
export type CompositionLineType = (typeof COMPOSITION_LINE_TYPES)[number]

export const SPIRAL_CORNERS = ['topLeft', 'topRight', 'bottomLeft', 'bottomRight'] as const
export type SpiralCorner = (typeof SPIRAL_CORNERS)[number]

export interface GridLines {
  readonly on: boolean
  readonly cols: number // integer MIN_GRID..MAX_GRID
  readonly rows: number // integer MIN_GRID..MAX_GRID
}
export interface SpiralLines {
  readonly on: boolean
  readonly corner: SpiralCorner // where the outer arc starts (M3-R10)
}
export interface LineStyle {
  readonly colour: string // '#rrggbb', lowercase
  readonly widthMm: number // MIN_LINE_WIDTH_MM..MAX_LINE_WIDTH_MM, a multiple of LINE_WIDTH_STEP_MM
  readonly opacityPct: number // integer MIN_LINE_OPACITY_PCT..MAX_LINE_OPACITY_PCT
}
export interface LineSettings {
  readonly grid: GridLines
  readonly thirds: boolean
  readonly armature: boolean
  readonly golden: boolean
  readonly spiral: SpiralLines
  readonly centre: boolean
  readonly style: LineStyle
}

export const MIN_GRID = 1
export const MAX_GRID = 20
export const MIN_LINE_WIDTH_MM = 0.1
export const MAX_LINE_WIDTH_MM = 2
export const LINE_WIDTH_STEP_MM = 0.05
export const MIN_LINE_OPACITY_PCT = 10
export const MAX_LINE_OPACITY_PCT = 100

/** Owner Q6 default (design/lines.html): every type off (Q29), grid 4 × 5, spiral from the top left, #e0457b, 0.35 mm, 90 %. */
export const DEFAULT_LINES: LineSettings = {
  grid: { on: false, cols: 4, rows: 5 },
  thirds: false,
  armature: false,
  golden: false,
  spiral: { on: false, corner: 'topLeft' },
  centre: false,
  style: { colour: '#e0457b', widthMm: 0.35, opacityPct: 90 },
}

/** Total and idempotent (M3-R16). Any input with the right shape becomes valid; NaN or unknown values take the default field. */
export function sanitizeLines(lines: LineSettings): LineSettings
export function linesEqual(a: LineSettings, b: LineSettings): boolean

export interface LinesPatch {
  readonly grid?: Partial<GridLines>
  readonly thirds?: boolean
  readonly armature?: boolean
  readonly golden?: boolean
  readonly spiral?: Partial<SpiralLines>
  readonly centre?: boolean
  readonly style?: Partial<LineStyle>
}
/** Merge (grid, spiral and style one level deep; undefined = not patched), then sanitize. */
export function patchLines(lines: LineSettings, patch: LinesPatch): LineSettings

/** The switched-on types, in COMPOSITION_LINE_TYPES order. */
export function activeLineTypes(lines: LineSettings): readonly CompositionLineType[]
/**
 * Stable string of what prints: '-' when no type is on; otherwise the active types with their
 * parameters, then the style, e.g. 'g4x5,t,phi,s:topLeft|#e0457b|0.35|90'. Injective over sanitized
 * settings that print something; ignores the parameters of types that are off.
 */
export function linesKey(lines: LineSettings): string
/** Every type off; grid size, spiral corner and style kept (owner Q7 default: what new photos inherit). */
export function withoutLineTypes(lines: LineSettings): LineSettings
```

export type PathCmd =
  | { readonly op: 'M'; readonly x: Mm; readonly y: Mm }

  readonly h: Mm

export const GOLDEN_FRACTIONS: readonly [number, number] // [1/φ², 1/φ] (M3-R11)
export function gridPaths(cols: number, rows: number, frame: FrameSize): PathCmd[] // M3-R13
export function thirdsPaths(frame: FrameSize): PathCmd[]
export function armaturePaths(frame: FrameSize): PathCmd[] // M3-R12
export function goldenPaths(frame: FrameSize): PathCmd[]
export function centrePaths(frame: FrameSize): PathCmd[]
export function centreDashMm(widthMm: Mm): readonly [Mm, Mm] // M3-R14

// place.ts (A2)
export function frameOf(trim: RectMm, turned: boolean): FrameSize // M3-R3
export function frameToPage(cmd: PathCmd, trim: RectMm, turned: boolean): PathCmd // M3-R3

// spiral.ts (A3)
export const KAPPA: number // 4/3·(√2 − 1)
export const SPIRAL_ARCS = 12

```

Subpath order is part of the contract (M3-R15, it fixes the PDF bytes):

- `gridPaths`: verticals left to right, then horizontals top to bottom, each `M` (top or left end) + `L`.
- `thirdsPaths` / `goldenPaths`: the two verticals left to right, then the two horizontals top to bottom.
- `armaturePaths`: diagonal TL→BR, diagonal TR→BL; then TL→(w, h/2), TL→(w/2, h), TR→(0, h/2), TR→(w/2, h), BL→(w, h/2), BL→(w/2, 0), BR→(0, h/2), BR→(w/2, 0); then the rhombus as one subpath `M (w/2, 0) L (w, h/2) L (w/2, h) L (0, h/2) L (w/2, 0)` (no close-path operator, so both renderers draw the same joins).
- `centrePaths`: the vertical (top to bottom), then the horizontal (left to right).

- **PDF (C2):** `PdfComposer.addPage` draws, after every image and before the crop marks, for each `TileLines`, exactly this operator sequence (pt, y flipped; via `pushOperators`):

  ```
  q
  <x> <y> <w> <h> re  W  n            clip = trim
  <r> <g> <b> RG                      colour / 255
  <widthPt> w  0 J  0 j               butt caps, miter joins
  /<GS> gs                            only when opacity < 1: an ExtGState { /CA opacity }

  Q

## Memory budgets (hard constraint)

A real iPhone 15 Pro Max killed the tab in M1 before #75; M2 kept every phase within a stated budget. Lines add **no pixels anywhere**: no canvas, bitmap, `ImageData` or decode. Their whole cost is plain path data in the page model and a few kilobytes of PDF content stream. M3 states that and pins it, so a later change that rasterises lines (or re-renders tiles when lines change) fails a test.

| Phase | What M3 adds | Bound | Pinned by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Images loaded, idle | a `lines` object per image (~10 fields) | < 1 KB per image | B1 unit (store holds plain data only) |
| Page models | per tile with lines: one `TileLines` with ≤ `MAX_LINE_CMDS_PER_TILE` = 200 path commands (a 20 × 20 grid is 76, every other type together 58 more: thirds 8, armature 25, golden 8, spiral 13, centre 4) | < 32 KB per tile; 66 tiles < 2.2 MB, rebuilt (not accumulated) on each pipeline run | C1 unit (the worst case is under the bound; property: any settings stay under it) |
| Preview | no bitmap or canvas; strokes are drawn on the existing sheet canvas | 0 | C3 unit: drawing lines creates no canvas and calls no `drawImage`; toggling lines changes no `tileRenderKey`, so no tile canvas or study job is created (C1, C3) |
| Export | page models (with lines) cloned into the worker; PDF operators per tile (≈ 6 KB worst case) | < 1 MB for 66 tiles; 0 extra decodes, encodes or embeds | C2 unit: lines on/off embed the same images, same count; D3 E2E: image XObjects byte-identical |
| Phone E2E | memory test M3 extended: every composition line on for all 22 photos (via "Apply lines to all") × 3 versions | unchanged budgets: import / studies-phase and settled < 1500 MB, export peak < 1700 MB | D4 |

If D4 exceeds a budget, that is a regression to fix, not a budget to raise without the owner (Q-budget).

## Testing strategy (spec §7, M3 additions)

- **Model (A1, node, Vitest + fast-check):** `sanitizeLines` total and idempotent (property, including garbage values, NaN, wrong hex, unknown corner); ranges and the 0.05 mm rounding; `patchLines` merges one level deep; `linesEqual`; `activeLineTypes` order; `linesKey` injective over sanitized settings that print (property: different printing settings ⇒ different key) and `'-'` whenever nothing is on, whatever the parameters; `withoutLineTypes` keeps style, grid size and corner.
- **Geometry (A2, A3, node, example + property tests):**
  - thirds at exactly w/3, 2w/3, h/3, 2h/3 (spec §7); golden lines at w/φ² and w/φ (≈ 0.382 / 0.618; M3-R11, the mockup's 114.6 / 185.4 of 300); centre at w/2, h/2; grid `cols − 1` verticals at k·w/cols and `rows − 1` horizontals, none on the edges, 1 × 1 → none; armature: 10 segments + 1 rhombus subpath, each corner line ends at the stated midpoint, the figure is symmetric under both mirrors (property).
  - **Every point and every Bézier control point lies inside the frame** for every type, corner and random frame 1–1000 mm (property; with the convex-hull property this proves the spiral stays within bounds, spec §7).
  - Spiral: starts exactly at the chosen corner and leaves it along the short edge (the first control point lies on that edge); 12 arcs; each arc starts where the previous ended (C0) with a parallel tangent (G1); arc radii shrink by 1/φ per arc in canonical space; the first arc ends on the golden line of the long side; the four corners are the mirror images of one another; the 300 × 400 top-right case reproduces the mockup's end points (300, 0) → (0, 247.2) → (185.4, 400) → (300, 305.6) → (229.2, 247.2) → (185.4, 283.3) within 0.05; a Bézier quarter arc stays within 0.03 % of the true ellipse (sampled).
  - Frame mapping: not turned = translation; turned maps the frame's corners TL, TR, BR, BL onto the trim's TR, BR, BL, TL (90° clockwise, as `combineRotation`); a property test inverts the map; `frameOf` swaps w and h when turned.
- **State (B1, B2, B3):** store `updateLines` no-op identity, `applyLinesToAll` copies everything and counts, records `appliedLines`, never touches `study` or `edits`, and `applyStudyToAll` never touches `lines` (D7, both directions); new imports take the default lines at the end of decode; settings v2 → v3 keeps every v2 field and gains defaults, v1 still loads, one bad `lineDefaults` field keeps the others, ranges clamp exactly as `sanitizeLines`, `partialize` persists `lineDefaults`; layout: two identical photos with different lines give the same layout in either input order; no lines anywhere → golden snapshot unchanged.
- **Render (C1–C3):**
  - Page model: golden snapshot of a page with a 3-version group and every type on (one entry per tile, same frame-relative geometry on each tile); a turned placement (spiral from the top left starts at the trim's top right); user rotation and flips do **not** move the lines (M3-R2: a photo rotated 90° by the user with a top-left spiral still starts at the trim's top left); clip = trim, never trim + bleed; no entry when nothing is on; `tileRenderKey` identical with lines on and off; the command bound.
  - PDF: the exact operator sequence for one `TileLines` (text snapshot of the content stream), opacity → ExtGState only below 100 %, dashed batch after the solid batch, coordinates in pt with y flipped, Béziers as `c`; a page without lines is byte-identical to M2's output; round trip through the new `PdfStroke` parser gives back the page-model geometry within 0.001 mm; crop-mark counts unchanged with lines on (`countStrokedLines` counts registration strokes only); the parser handles q/Q nesting, `cm` (image draws in between), clip and gs.
  - Preview: the recorded canvas calls replay `page.lines` exactly (clip rect, every cmd scaled by `pxPerMm`, dash scaled, width floored at 1 device px only when thinner, globalAlpha, caps/joins), lines after tiles and before crop marks, drawn whether guides are on or off; no `drawImage` and no canvas creation for lines.
- **UI (D1, D2):** Testing Library behaviour tests for every control (switch toggles, the revealed grid and corner controls, `CountField` clamping and arrow keys, `ColourField` hex, sliders' value text), "Apply lines to all" announcement and count, disabled while importing (with the shared hook, StudiesPanel's tests still pass unchanged), the Lines tab, the phone `<details>` summary with "N on", `LineDefaultsEffect`, `describePage` line types.

### New questions raised by M3

Q1. **What does "Diagonals & armature" draw?** The spec names "diagonals / armature of the rectangle"; the mockup draws the two diagonals, six lines from corners to the middle of the far sides, and a diamond through the side midpoints.
   _Recommendation:_ the symmetric version of the mockup: the 2 diagonals, all **8** corner-to-far-side-midpoint lines, and the diamond (14 lines). Alternatives: (b) also add the 4 "reciprocal" diagonals (each corner's perpendicular to the main diagonal); (c) the 2 diagonals only.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q2. **Golden spiral on a photo that isn't golden-shaped** (most photos: 3:2, 4:3, 1:1).
   _Recommendation:_ stretch the spiral to fill the whole picture (as mocked). It then passes through the golden-ratio lines' crossings and always reaches the picture's edges; its arcs become slightly oval. The alternative is a true-proportion spiral that fits inside the picture and leaves a strip uncovered.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q3. **"Spiral starts at" a corner — which way does it go?** From any corner the spiral can run two ways.
   _Recommendation:_ as mocked: its widest arc starts at the chosen corner and first runs along the picture's **short** edge, then winds inward towards the golden-ratio crossing on the far side (from the top right of a portrait photo it tightens near the lower right). Four choices, one per corner.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q4. **Lines and photo edits.** Do lines follow the picture as you edited it (crop, rotation, flips)?
   _Recommendation:_ yes. Lines describe the printed picture as you see it: "top left" is the top left of the picture after your edits, the grid's columns run across its width, and rotating the photo doesn't move the lines on it. When the layout turns a photo sideways to pack it, its lines turn with it.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q5. **Lines and bleed.** With bleed on, the picture's edge pixels extend 3 mm past the cut line.
   _Recommendation:_ lines stop at the cut line (the picture's real edge); they never run into the bleed, the gutter or the crop marks.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q6. **Defaults and ranges** (none in the spec; these are the mockup's).
   _Recommendation:_ every line type off for a new photo (spec), grid 4 columns × 5 rows, spiral from the top left, colour **#e0457b** (the mockup's pink), thickness **0.35 mm**, opacity **90%**; ranges: grid 1–20 each way, thickness 0.1–2 mm in 0.05 mm steps, opacity 10–100%. (Note: on screen the bleed guide is a similar magenta; guides are screen-only and can be hidden.)
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q7. **Which line settings are remembered between visits?** Spec §2.9 says "default … line settings" are saved.
   _Recommendation:_ the **last used** colour, thickness, opacity, grid size and spiral corner are remembered and given to newly added photos; the **line types are not** (new photos start with no lines, spec Q29), like study versions in M2. "Apply lines to all" counts as last used (as M2-1).
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q8. **What does "Apply lines to all images" copy?**
   _Recommendation:_ everything on the Lines tab — which types are on, grid size, spiral corner, colour, thickness and opacity — to every image, never studies, crop or copies (D7). No confirmation; the result is announced ("Line settings copied to 6 images"), as in M2 Q7.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q9. **Lines controls while photos are importing.** In M2 (M2-2) the Studies controls wait for imports to finish, so "Apply to all" can't miss a late photo.
   _Recommendation:_ the same rule for Lines: disabled with the "Waiting for photos to finish importing…" hint while any photo is importing.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q10. **Telling line types apart.** All types share one colour per image.
   _Recommendation:_ as mocked: centre lines dashed, every other type solid, one colour, thickness and opacity per image. Per-type colours could come in M5.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q11. **Thickness unit.** The app switches between mm and inches.
   _Recommendation:_ line thickness always in **mm** (like pen sizes: 0.35 mm), even when the page unit is inches.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q12. **Button wording.** On the phone, the Studies step will show both "Apply to all images" (studies) and the Lines section's button, on one screen.
   _Recommendation:_ the Lines button reads **"Apply lines to all images"** on desktop and phone, so the two can't be confused (the mockup says "Apply to all images" on the Lines tab). The studies button keeps its wording.
   **A:** accepted (recommended default). Owner, 2026-10-07.

Q-budget. **Memory and speed budgets.** The CI memory test (22 × 24 MP photos × 3 versions, now with every line on) keeps the budgets: under 1500 MB after import and while studies render, under 1700 MB during export; a line change updates the preview in under 200 ms.
