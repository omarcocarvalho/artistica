### Task E2: Centre-line dashes that fit small tiles

**Branch:** `fix/lines-centre-dash` · **PR title:** `fix(lines): scale the centre-line dash with the tile` · **Depends on:** —

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests** (`geometry.test.ts`, `tile-lines.test.ts`):
  - `centreDashMm(w, short)` (M5-R20): the M3 pattern `[max(1.5, 6w), max(1, 4w)]` when its period is at most `short / 3`; otherwise both parts scaled down so the period is `short / 3`, but the period never below `max(2.5, 2.5w)`; table: (0.35, 100) → [2.1, 1.4] (M3's), (2, 100) → [12, 8], (2, 12) → [3, 2], (2, 6) → [3, 2] (the floor), (0.1, 3) → [1.5, 1] (the M3 floors already give the minimum period); add more rows in the test and check each by hand in the PR;

  - the M3 golden snapshots are unchanged (their tiles are large).
- [ ] **Step 2: RED, implement, GREEN.**
- [ ] **Step 3: E2E:** L-X1 (`e2e/lines.spec.ts`) gains a 12 mm tile at 2 mm width whose PDF dash array equals `centreDashMm(2, 12)`, and the preview's `setLineDash` call equals it scaled.
