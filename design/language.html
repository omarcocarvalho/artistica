Choosing the language

A globe in the app's top bar, at every width. Choosing a language switches the whole app at once and remembers the choice on this device. The bar at the top of this page is live: pick a language to see the name change.

A language fails to load

Each language other than English loads when chosen. If that fails (offline before it was ever loaded), the app keeps the language it shows, puts the select back on it and shows an error toast in that language. Focus stays on the select.

English

Couldn't load that language. Check your connection and try again.

What a screen reader hears

The globe and the visible name are hidden from assistive technology; the select carries both the name and the value.

  • Tab to it: “Language, English, pop-up button” (wording varies by screen reader).
  • Open it: the seven options, each read in its own language.
  • Choose 日本語: the page switches at once, <html lang> becomes ja, and focus stays on the select, now “言語, 日本語”.
  • Messages already on screen keep the language they were written in until they go.
