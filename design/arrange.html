Arrange by hand

An explicit Arrange mode on the preview. A photo moves with all its study versions as one block. Drag to move (also onto another page), drop on another photo to swap, drag a corner to resize (the shape is kept). Photos keep 20 mm on their short side, stay inside the margins and a gutter apart. Every drag has a keyboard and a single-pointer way. Outlines on the sheet use fixed colours with 3:1 on white, because the sheet is white in both themes.

Toolbar, Arrange off

Outside Arrange mode the preview is as today: a click or tap selects a photo. Undo and Re-run are off while the layout is automatic.

Toolbar, Arrange on, a photo selected

Arrange is a pressed toggle. The selected photo gets the single-pointer controls: Move to page (pages and “New page”), Swap with… (the other photos by name and page), Width (in the user's unit, applied on Enter or when leaving the field).

Dragging: a valid spot

The ghost follows the pointer (a CSS transform, no redraw); the photo's old place is dotted and faded. Edges within 2 mm of the margin or of a neighbour plus the gutter snap. Release to place it.
