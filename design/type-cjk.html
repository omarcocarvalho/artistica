Japanese, Korean and Chinese type

No font is downloaded for these languages: each uses the fonts every supported system ships, with its own list, so Chinese text never takes its Han characters from a Japanese font. Latin letters keep the app's fonts; headings and the hand-written accents use the language's sans-serif for CJK characters (no serif or handwriting CJK font), and no bold or italic is faked.

Line breaking

Each box is 12 characters wide. Japanese and Chinese use line-break: strict: a line never starts with 。 or 、 or a small kana, so the character before moves down with it. Korean breaks at spaces like English (word-break: keep-all), never inside a word. The right-hand boxes show what the rule prevents; the app never uses them.
