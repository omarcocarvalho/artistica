How the phone flow works

  • Five steps, one task each. The step bar is also tappable, so you can jump back.
  • Settings and the live preview are the same engine as desktop; Preview is a swipeable page carousel.
  • Per-image edit opens as a bottom sheet (tap any thumbnail in step 1). Under the crop, Position and Size buttons (44 px) move and resize the crop one step per tap, as the arrow keys and Shift + arrows do, so cropping needs no drag.
  • Lines sit inside the Studies step (collapsed) to keep the five steps from the spec.
  • All targets ≥ 44 px; inputs use 16 px text so iOS doesn't zoom.
  • Export runs page by page with progress, to keep phone memory low. It is inline in the Export step: one “Create PDF”, no dialog, and no Export button in the top bar.
  • Preview has an Arrange toolbar under the pages (44 px). In Arrange mode a photo is dragged by touch (the carousel still scrolls from the gaps), corner handles have 44 px hit areas, and “Photo options” opens a sheet with Move to page, Swap with…, Width and Position (1 mm steps), so every drag also works with taps. Moving to another page uses Move to page (the other pages are off screen). The second phone shows that state.
