A proper logo

Three directions on the “sketchbook on a warm studio desk” concept. Each mark is one SVG on a 32-unit grid with no text in it; the word “Artistica” beside it is live text in Fraunces. Every mark works in a single colour: in forced colours it takes the system text and background colours, and the one-colour version needs no second ink. The sheet stays white on dark backgrounds, as the printed page does in the app.

Recommendation: A, Sheet and spiral. It is used for v0.5.0 (top bar, landing page, favicon, home-screen icon and share image); the owner chooses at the v1.0.0 sign-off, and another direction is a small change.

A. Sheet and spiral Recommended

A sheet from the sketchbook, tilted as if just put on the desk, with one photo tile and a golden-spiral line over it: the three things the app does (pack, study, draw lines) in one shape. It keeps the placeholder's tilt and colours, so it reads as a refinement rather than a change of brand. At 16 px the sheet and the tile still read; the spiral becomes a light accent.
