How it works

Three steps, under a minute.

  1. Add photos

    Upload, drag in, paste, or use a link. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and iPhone HEIC.

  2. Pick your paper

    A4, Letter and more. Artistica finds a layout with the fewest pages and the biggest images.

  3. Print & cut

    Download a PDF with crop marks and optional bleed. Print at 100% and cut along the marks.

Made to help you see

  • Smart packing

    Sizes each photo for drawing, never past what its pixels allow at 300 DPI.

  • Crop marks and bleed

    Printer-style marks, optional bleed, and a safe area, so cutting is easy.

  • Edit each photo

    Crop, rotate, flip, print copies, or fix an exact size.

  • Works on your phone

    A step-by-step flow on small screens, side panels on desktop, light and dark.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source (MIT).

Will my prints be the right size?

Yes, if you print at 100% (“Actual size”). Pages are made at the exact paper size you pick.

Why won't an image link work?

Some sites block other apps from reading their images. Download the image and upload it instead.

What do “bleed” and “crop marks” mean?

Crop marks show where to cut. Bleed prints a little extra past the cut, so a slightly-off cut leaves no white edge.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Photos are never uploaded or saved, and refreshing the page clears them.

What finds the face and pose guides?

Face and pose guides use Google's MediaPipe models, running on your device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes: latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop, iPhone and Android.
